# Skills

Techniques, areas of competence and judgement: what a player gets better at,
as distinct from what a partnership agrees. One directory per ID,
`<category>/<name>/skill.toml`, and the path is the ID.

| `kind` | |
|---|---|
| `skill` | A technique or an area of competence: finessing, choosing an opening lead, slam bidding |
| `judgement` | How a bidder decides: hand evaluation, the Law of Total Tricks, penalty doubles |

Agreements are not here. A convention or a natural method is in
[conventions/](../conventions/), whose IDs share one namespace with these.
Where something is both, as leads and signals are, the skill is defined
here and the agreement is the card's settings, which name the skill.

A skill has the keys a definition has (the contract, section 4), without
treatments or functions. The categories' display names are in
`conventions/categories.toml`.
